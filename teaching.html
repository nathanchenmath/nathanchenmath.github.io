Course Description

This is a course on birational transformation groups of algebraic varieties. Topics include: the Cremona group for the projective plane and some of its subgroups, Sarkisov links for surfaces, dynamical invariants of birational transformations, topologies on birational transformation groups, b-classes, and Zariski-Riemann spaces.

Prerequisites: Basic knowledge of algebraic curves and surfaces.
